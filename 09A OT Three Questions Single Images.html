Three Questions

Every month we’ll take a few minutes to get to know one of our esteemed colleagues at the Center for Academic Innovation. 
This month we’re talking with Jane Doe, our chief of LEGO building and implementation.

Q: Jane, what was the first LEGO set you completed?
A: It was the Death Star from Star Wars. It took me almost the entire holiday break, and I proudly displayed it in my room until well after high school graduation. I was a huge Darth Vader fan.

 

Q: Why is LEGO still relevant to learners today?
A: They remain at the intersection of STEM and creativity. You can dream up scenarios like flying cars, and then engineer a design that captures your creation.

 

Q: Who is the best LEGO movie character?
A: Hands down, Wyldstyle.
